About Us

A small kitchen with a long memory

Delicious Bites started in 2019 as a six-table room with one wood-fired oven and a very short menu. We still cook the same way: buy what looks good that morning, cook it simply, and serve it while it is hot.

Our team of eleven shares one rule — if we would not serve it to our own family, it does not leave the kitchen. That is the whole philosophy, and it has not changed since the first service.
